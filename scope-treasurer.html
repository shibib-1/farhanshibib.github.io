Overview

I joined SCOPE as a general member in fall 2025, working on the club's UAV project — helping with ArduPilot integration, electromagnet payload control, and the power delivery chain for the drone. In spring 2026 I was promoted to Treasurer and Project Lead.

The technical work on the drone platform still shows up under Projects on this site — this page is about the other half of the role: running the club's budget and keeping our projects moving.
