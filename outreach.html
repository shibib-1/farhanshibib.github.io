SCOPE Outreach · April 2026

First Aid &
Emergency Preparedness

✓ Completed Temple Terrace Elementary Tampa, FL ~30 Students Reached

Our SCOPE team stepped outside the lab and into a 4th grade classroom — spending a full day teaching kids what to actually do when emergencies happen. Not textbook theory. Real scenarios, hands-on kits, and skills they walked out the door with.

The Day

When most people think about engineering, they picture labs and circuit boards. This event was about something different — using what we know to genuinely help people who need it. We partnered with Temple Terrace Elementary, one of Hillsborough County's community schools, and spent an afternoon with their 4th graders learning about emergencies together.

The session covered three core scenarios: what to do when there's a fire, how to prepare for and respond to a hurricane, and how to handle general medical emergencies. For each one, we broke down the STEM behind it — why smoke rises, how storm surge works, what happens to your body under stress — in language that made it click for a 9-year-old.

The highlight was the hands-on portion. Every student assembled their own first aid kit from scratch — bandages, gauze, instructions — and got to decorate the box to make it theirs. They left with something real, something they could actually use. At the end of the session, each student received a training certificate. You could see how much that meant to them.

Moments like this are a reminder of why we're in engineering in the first place. Skills only matter if they reach people.

What We Covered
🔥
Fire Safety

Escape planning, stop-drop-roll, and the science of how heat and smoke move through a building.

🌀
Hurricane Response

Storm timelines, shelter-in-place decisions, what belongs in an emergency bag, and how to stay safe before power goes out.

🚑
General Emergencies

When to call 911, how to describe a location clearly, and basic first-response awareness every kid should have.

🧰
DIY First Aid Kits

Each student built and decorated their own kit — bandages, gauze, and instructions — to take home and keep.

📜
Training Certificates

Every participant earned a Red Cross–style certificate at the end of the session, acknowledging their completion.

🤝
~30 Students

A full class of 4th graders engaged start to finish — asking questions, laughing, and actually retaining it.
