Overview

I started as an RA at Beta Hall and moved to Kosove Hall for the 2026–2027 year. It's not an engineering role, but it's the one that's taught me the most about actually showing up for people — being reliable, communicating clearly, and staying calm when something goes wrong at 11pm on a duty night.

I'm CLASP certified through RA training, and I hold every part of the job the same way I'd hold a lab report: document it, follow through, and don't cut corners.

What I Actually Do
  • Plan and run community events — including a volleyball event I organized with another RA that pulled a strong turnout for our floor's first social of the year.
  • Design and build bulletin boards and printed materials for the hall.
  • Give presentations to residents and run floor meetings.
  • Keep closing spreadsheets and room-check records updated and accurate.
  • Submit and track maintenance work orders for residents.
  • Cover shifts and support other RAs when something comes up — swapping duty nights, grabbing the duty bag for someone running late, that kind of thing.
